## The project

I designed and led this undergraduate research project at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, Xiamen University Malaysia, from April 2024 to January 2025. The system is a charging unit powered by renewable energy from a microgrid of wind turbines and photovoltaic panels, paired with a digital twin for remote monitoring.

## How it works

On the physical side, I wrote the solar-tracking firmware in C for an ESP32 microcontroller, integrating photoelectric sensors so the panels follow the sun at an efficient angle. On the digital side, I built 3D models of the photovoltaic components in SolidWorks and simulated their operation in a Unity-based digital twin. The twin mirrors the microgrid in real time for monitoring and supports performance analysis of its solar and wind sources.

## Why it matters

A digital twin makes a renewable installation observable without anyone on site, which keeps monitoring and maintenance costs low. The project ran alongside my 2024 papers on digital-twin systems for human–robot collaboration and smart farming, which examine the same technology in other application areas.
